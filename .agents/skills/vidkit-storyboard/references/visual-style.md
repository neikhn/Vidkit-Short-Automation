# Visual style
Reference observations: 2026-09-23. Guidance reviewed: 2026-09-25.

[Beeknoee AI](https://www.youtube.com/@BeeknoeeAI/shorts) sampled dark gradients, logo-led hooks and charts. [AI NEWS](https://www.youtube.com/@AINEWS-AIDailyNews/shorts) sampled source-page footage and typographic explainers. These are visual observations, not proof of production tools or performance causality. Do not copy channel branding or scripts.

Select a theme after research: `dark-grid` for technical/API topics by default, `dark-contours` for research or model explanations, or `light-editorial` when product identity/content supports it. Theme is explicit data; do not force one look on every topic. A subtle moving pattern may support the scene; decoration must not compete with reading.

For v7-v9 videos, also choose a separate global background from Grid Drift, Contour Flow, Light Editorial, Radial Glow or Fireflies. Pick it for the explanation and theme colors, then inspect its library still and two-loop clip before putting `backgroundId` in the brief. It moves on the absolute video timeline across scene cuts. Keep any custom scene's full-frame fill transparent unless intentional; inspect readability behind the primary visual and captions at 360×640. Existing videos retain their background behavior.

Design the explanation first: what appears, what changes, what the viewer learns, and what continues into the next beat. Brand hook, screenshot focus, API response reveal, diagram flow, metric breakdown and takeaway are starting points, not mandatory scene families. Reuse, compose or write a custom TSX visual according to the idea. Place logo or a real hero visual in the hook when available. Without brand imagery, use the product name and an explanatory graphic, never a fabricated logo. Prefer focused crops and one claim per scene. Show official media by its true type; a chart or artwork is not a console screenshot.

Motion should reveal meaning: focus an image region, spotlight a feature, reveal a verified JSON field, trace a flow, or emphasize a sourced number. Keep source-image crops proportional. Avoid three near-identical layouts in a row, tiny landscape charts in a portrait frame, generic watermarks and empty space without intent.

Do not add corrective overlays such as “Sơ đồ minh họa · Không phải kết quả chạy model” or “Illustration · Not measured model output.” Keep provenance, evidence type and limitations in source pack, brief and QA. Make schematic visuals self-evidently schematic; never depict invented model measurements or simulated UI as verified results. If the distinction is material to comprehension, explain it naturally in narration or redesign the visual instead of adding a disclaimer strip. Preserve concise on-screen source attribution where it supports a factual claim.

Target 1080×1920 at 30 fps. Check a 360×640 preview, platform safe areas, Vietnamese glyphs, long names and caption balance. After choosing the visual idea, use `vidkit library search/show/preview` to assess reusable material. If none fits, implement a job-specific visual; promotion is optional and independent of video approval. Imported Remotion Elements/Templates require recorded source, license and version.

For new v6 jobs, identify the primary visual group in each scene before placing it. Center the whole logo, card cluster, chart or diagram horizontally within the visual area, independent of the left-aligned title, heading and source. Text inside a centered card or chart may remain left-aligned for readability. Keep vertical placement driven by the scene and leave room for captions. Custom visuals should follow the same rule without being forced into a built-in layout; inspect the group's apparent center at the start and end of its motion.


For v10, use a required pattern and optional transparent Radial Glow/Fireflies layers. Read [layered background design](../../vidkit-pipeline/references/background-design.md) for selection, props and the still-first candidate review. Simple grids, dots and lines are first-class choices; the three supplied references do not define the entire palette.
